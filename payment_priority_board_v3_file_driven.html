Calculation logic

Mandatory risk first
Then priority score
Then lost revenue/day

The technical team only fills the request details and verbal risk dropdowns. The app resolves company and asset metadata from the masters, calculates daily production/revenue loss from the asset type plus economics config, and then keeps an open ranking for the economic team.

Lost kWh/day = Capacity(MW) × 1000 × 24 × TypicalCF × ProductionEffect%
Lost revenue/day = Lost kWh/day × Value_per_kWh
Security risk = Likelihood × Impact, HSE risk = Likelihood × Impact
Simple payback (days) = Request amount / Lost revenue/day
