SCH Payment Priority Board

Technical submits the issue. The app derives the asset economics, calculates lost kWh/day, lost revenue/day, payback, and risk, then ranks all open requests so the economic department can decide what to pay first.

The app ships with an embedded copy of the current asset/company masters and a default economics config. State is saved locally in your browser.

Ranking logic

Mandatory risk first Then score per euro Then lost revenue/day

The score is inspired by the workbook logic: high daily exposure, high Security/HSE risk, and lower payment amount move a request upward. Paid requests are archived and automatically removed from the live ranking.

Formula summary
Lost kWh/day = Capacity(MW) × 1000 × 24 × CFtyp × ProductionEffect%
Lost revenue/day = Lost kWh/day × Value€/kWh
SecRisk = Likelihood × Impact, HseRisk = Likelihood × Impact
Priority score = ((eps+nExposure)^wE × (eps+nSec)^wS × (eps+nHse)^wH) / ((eps+nPayment)^wP)
Simple payback = Request amount / Lost revenue/day
