Calculation logic

Business critical / high risk first
Then stable absolute score
Then exposure/day and age

The technical team fills only the request and risk details. The app resolves company and asset metadata from the masters, calculates production/revenue loss from the selected asset plus economics config, and then keeps a stable ranked queue for the economic team.

Lost kWh/day = Capacity(MW) × 1000 × 24 × TypicalCF × ProductionEffect%
Lost revenue/day = Lost kWh/day × Value_per_kWh
Priority class = Critical business obligation override, else Security/HSE risk thresholds
Stable score = exposure/day + Security risk + HSE risk + 30-day avoided-loss efficiency - mild size penalty
30-day efficiency = (Exposure/day × 30) / Request amount
Simple payback (days) = Request amount / Lost revenue/day
